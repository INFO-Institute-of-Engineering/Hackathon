AI-Powered Disaster Prediction & Safe Evacuation

An AI-based system that analyzes data such as weather, sensors, and past disaster information to predict possible disasters early and help people evacuate safely through the best available routes.

02 AI

AI Compliance & Filing Anomaly Detector

Develop an AI system that analyzes financial and regulatory documents to detect errors, inconsistencies, anomalies, and potential compliance violations before they result in penalties.

03 AI

AI Crime Pattern & Risk Analysis

Build an AI system that analyzes historical crime data and maps to identify patterns, hotspots, and time-based trends to support effective resource deployment.

04 AI

AI Prompt Optimizer

Develop an AI-powered prompt optimization tool that analyzes user prompts, identifies weaknesses, and generates improved versions to produce more accurate, relevant, and consistent AI responses.

05 AI

AI-Powered Quantum Algorithm Tutor

Develop an AI-powered learning platform that simplifies basic quantum algorithms through personalized explanations, interactive quantum circuit visualizations, step-by-step guidance, and short practice exercises for college studens.

06 AI

Deepfake Voice and Video KYC Fraud Detector

Develop an AI system that analyzes voice and video inputs to detect AI generated or manipulated content during KYC verification, helping identify potential identity fraud.

07 AI

Al-Based Crop Disease Detection

Develop an Al model that analyzes crop images and detects diseases early, helping farmers take timely corrective actions.

08 AI

AI-Powered Artisan-to-Market Intelligence Platform

An AI-powered mobile app for artisans that converts product photos and voice inputs into professional product listings, suggests optimal pricing, and connects them with online marketplaces and buyers.

09 AI

AI-Powered Hyperlocal Air Quality Prediction

Develop an AI system that predicts neighborhood-level air quality using weather, traffic, and environmental data, and generates timely alerts and safety recommendations for schools, healthcare centers, and the public.

10 AI

AI-Powered UPI Dispute Detection & Resolution

Develop an AI system that analyzes UPI transaction and complaint data to classify disputes such as fraud, non-delivery, or incorrect amounts, and recommends quick, explainable resolutions for low-risk cases.

11 IoT for Automation

Smart Electrical Fault Detection & Automatic Isolation.

Develop an IoT-based system that continuously monitors electrical parameters such as voltage, current, power consumption, and temperature to detect abnormal conditions including overloads, short circuits, overheating, and electrical faults. The system should identify the affected circuit and automatically isolate it using appropriate switching mechanisms while sending real-time alerts to maintenance personnel.

12 IoT for Automation

Intelligent Water Distribution Pressure Management for Apartment Communities

Develop an IoT system that monitors water pressure, tank levels, pump operation, and flow rates across different apartment blocks. The system should identify uneven distribution or pressure problems and intelligently control pumps to ensure reliable water supply.

13 IoT for Automation

Automated Scrap-Sorting Line with Physical Routing

Develop an IoT-based automated sorting system that identifies different waste materials such as metal, plastic, paper, and other recyclable materials using sensors or AI. The system should physically route each material into the appropriate collection container, improving sorting speed and consistency.

14 IoT for Automation

Forest Fire Detection & Auto Alert Network

Develop an IoT-based system that uses temperature, smoke, and gas sensors to detect early signs of forest fires. The system should automatically trigger alerts, identify the affected area, and activate a small-scale automated suppression mechanism to reduce fire spread.

15 IoT for Automation

Livestock Health Monitoring & Geo-Fencing

Develop an IoT-based wearable system for cattle and sheep that monitors body temperature, heart rate, activity levels, and grazing location. The system should detect early signs of illness or distress, alert farmers about animals straying beyond geo-fenced boundaries, and help track livestock health history to reduce loss.

16 IoT for Automation

IoT-Based Smart Traffic Violation & Accident Response System

Develop an IoT-enabled system using cameras and sensors to detect traffic violations and accidents in real time. The system should automatically record violation details, identify accident locations, alert emergency services, and optimize traffic signals to provide a clear route for emergency vehicles.

17 IoT for Automation

IoT-Based Fuel Theft Prevention System for Vehicles

Fuel theft in personal and commercial vehicles leads to financial loss and inefficiency in transport/logistics sectors.

18 IoT for Automation

Emergency-Vehicle Green Corridor Automation

Develop an IoT-based system that detects emergency vehicles and automatically controls traffic signals to create a clear green corridor, reducing emergency response time.

19 IoT for Automation

Smart Water Leakage Localization & Automatic Shut-Off

Develop an IoT-based system that detects abnormal water-flow patterns and identifies the probable location of a leak in a building or small distribution network. The system should automatically isolate the affected section using motorized valves and continue supplying unaffected areas.

20 IoT for Automation

IoT-Based Smart Worker Safety & Machine Control System

Develop an IoT-based safety system that monitors worker movement around hazardous machines and restricted areas. When an unauthorized worker enters a danger zone, the system should automatically trigger an alert and, where appropriate, activate a machine safety mechanism to prevent accidents.

Smart Campus Infrastructure & Lost-and-Found Coordination System.

The system should reduce manual work and provide real time order status tracking for customers and pharmacy staff.

24 Full Stack

Supply Chain Disruption Intelligence System.

An intelligent supply chain monitoring platform that tracks suppliers, inventory, transport, and orders to detect disruptions like delays, shortages, and abnormal demand, delivering timely alerts and risk-mitigation insights.

Most safety apps are just an SOS button. They fail when the phone is locked, offline, or the user can't act openly. Build a platform that reduces risk beforehand and gets verified help quickly.

Legal Metrology officers can schedule inspections and issue digital verification certificates. The system improves accuracy, transparency, consumer protection, and reduces manual paperwork.
